# HALAL-INVEST: Product Roadmap (Stages 1 → 5)

## Stage 1: Core Investment Universe & Foundation (Current V1)
- Indian listed equities (NSE / BSE).
- Deterministic calculation engine for financial metrics & Shariah screening.
- Server-side FYERS broker adapter with manual order guidance.
- Provenance & evidence tracking engine.
- Responsive Web Dashboard, Company Research, Screener, Portfolio, Watchlist, Important Events.
- ₹0 fixed data cost architecture.

## Stage 2: Deep Corporate Action & Invalidation Engine
- Real-time corporate action adjustments (splits, bonuses, rights).
- Continuous monitoring of quarterly results and automated thesis invalidation checks.
- Notification dispatch on material risk or Shariah status changes.

## Stage 3: Multi-Market Expansion (Saudi Arabia & US)
- Tadawul (Saudi Arabia) market adapter.
- US Equities (NYSE/NASDAQ) adapter with SEC EDGAR ingestion.
- Multi-currency normalization (INR, SAR, USD).

## Stage 4: Multi-User Collaboration & Family Workspaces
- Workspaces for families and advisory groups.
- Row-Level Security (RLS) in PostgreSQL.
- Multi-broker support (Zerodha, Interactive Brokers, Al Rajhi Capital).

## Stage 5: Institutional Evidence Federation & Audit
- Cryptographically signed evidence snapshots.
- Public Shariah audit reports.
- Advanced scenario sensitivity stress testing.
