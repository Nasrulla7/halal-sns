# HALAL-INVEST: Investment Rules & Core Invariants

## Non-Negotiable Rules
1. **Never Fabricate Data:** If a number is unknown or unverified, state `Data unavailable` or `Insufficient evidence`. Never substitute zero.
2. **Never Fabricate Sources:** Every claim must identify its origin filing, disclosure, or mathematical formula.
3. **No Automatic Trading:** All executions are manually carried out by the user through their broker app.
4. **Shariah is a Hard Gate:** A company must pass both business activity and financial screening to be eligible for investment assessment.
5. **Conservative Sizing:**
   - Very Strong Conviction: 5% - 10%
   - Strong Conviction: 4% - 8%
   - Medium Conviction: 2% - 5%
   - Higher Risk / Speculative: 1% - 2%
   - Major Concerns / Non-Compliant: 0%
6. **Entry/Exit Discipline:** A high-quality company trading at unreasonable valuation yields `WAIT / INVESTIGATE`, never an uncritical buy.
