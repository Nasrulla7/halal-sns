# HALAL-INVEST: Assessment Framework

## Assessment Taxonomy
Assessments are conservative, disciplined, and evidence-grounded:
1. **BUY CANDIDATE:** Passes Shariah hard gate, demonstrates high business quality (high ROCE/ROE, low leverage, strong moat), reasonable valuation relative to growth, and no unmanaged red-team risks.
2. **WAIT / INVESTIGATE:** High business quality, compliant Shariah status, but valuation is elevated or pending key earnings clarity.
3. **HIGHER RISK / SPECULATIVE:** Compliant Shariah status, but higher business volatility, cyclical headwinds, or elevated debt approaching threshold.
4. **AVOID / NOT A CANDIDATE:** Fails Shariah hard gate, or exhibits severe accounting divergence, structural decline, or governance red flags.
5. **REQUIRES REVIEW:** Material event or regulatory action requires manual Shariah or forensic review.
6. **NO DECISION — INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** Critical financial statements, price freshness, or Shariah data missing. Mandatory fallback.

## Existing Holdings Assessment Taxonomy
- **HOLD:** Core thesis intact; valuation within reasonable bounds.
- **REVIEW:** Approaching valuation ceiling, slowing growth, or upcoming governance milestone.
- **CONSIDER REDUCING:** Exceeds maximum portfolio concentration limit (e.g. >10%), or valuation has expanded far beyond fundamentals.
- **SELL / EXIT CANDIDATE:** Shariah status breached, thesis broken, or persistent earnings-to-cash divergence detected.
