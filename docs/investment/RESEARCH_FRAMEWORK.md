# HALAL-INVEST: Research Framework

## Pipeline Stages
1. **Security Verification:** Symbol, ISIN, Exchange, Listing status.
2. **Shariah Hard Gate:** Business activity screen + financial thresholds.
3. **Business Model Analysis:** Moats, customer/supplier concentration, recurring revenue.
4. **Multi-Year Financial Audit:** 5-10 year Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow.
5. **Ratios & Margins:** P/E, P/B, ROE, ROCE, EBITDA margin, Net margin, CCC.
6. **Earnings Quality:** Operating Cash Flow vs Net Profit, inventory/receivable accruals.
7. **Valuation:** Multiples percentile, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, Bull/Base/Bear scenarios.
8. **Historical Market & Benchmarks:** Relative performance against NIFTY 50 / NIFTY 500.
9. **Peer Comparison:** Head-to-head comparison on margins, growth, and leverage.
10. **Management & Governance:** Promoter shareholding, pledging %, auditor tenure.
11. **Red-Teaming & Invalidation:** Finding reasons NOT to invest and thesis kill-switches.
12. **Safety Gate Verification:** Checks for missing or contradictory data.
13. **Assessment Emission:** Emits immutable assessment snapshot.
