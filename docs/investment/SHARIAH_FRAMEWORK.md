# HALAL-INVEST: Shariah Framework

## 1. Methodology Standards
Halal-Invest implements AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions) Shariah Standard No. (21) regarding investment in shares.

## 2. Business Activity Screening (Hard Gate 1)
The core business must be permissible. The following sectors and activities are strictly prohibited:
- Conventional banking, insurance, and lending with interest (Riba).
- Alcoholic beverages production and distribution.
- Pork and non-halal meat processing.
- Tobacco and narcotic manufacturing.
- Gambling, casinos, lotteries, and betting.
- Adult entertainment, pornography, and prostitution.
- Weapons and defense manufacturing intended for aggressive warfare.

**Threshold:** Any incidental non-permissible revenue must not exceed 5% of total revenue.

## 3. Financial Ratio Screening (Hard Gate 2)
Evaluated on the most recent 12-month consolidated financial filings:
1. **Debt Ratio:** Total interest-bearing debt / 36-month average market capitalization < 33%.
2. **Interest-Bearing Securities:** Total cash and interest-bearing deposits / 36-month average market capitalization < 33%.
3. **Receivables & Cash Ratio:** (Cash + Accounts Receivable) / Total Assets < 50% (Liquidity Screen).

## 4. Purification Requirement
If a company has incidental non-permissible income (e.g. 1.8% from interest on cash deposits or ancillary non-halal revenue), that percentage of any dividend received must be donated to charity without expectation of religious reward.
`Purification Amount = Dividend Received * Non-Permissible Income Percentage`
